Letter writing, in a world ruled by instant messaging and email, can feel almost forgotten. Yet that's exactly what makes it so powerful: when hardly anyone reaches for pen and paper anymore, a handwritten or carefully composed letter lands with a weight no notification will ever match. Anyone who finds an envelope in the mailbox understands it at once — someone chose to spend their time on them.

## Nostalgia Meets Modernity

Letter writing carries a certain nostalgia, evoking a time when people poured their thoughts onto paper, crafting each word with pen and ink. But that sentimentality doesn't make the letter obsolete. On the contrary: in an age of digital overload it's enjoying a quiet revival, helped along by apps that make composing and sending a real letter the work of a few taps.

## A Personal Touch in an Impersonal World

Between notifications, group chats, and overflowing inboxes, the personal is quickly lost. A letter you hold in your hands conveys a level of care that an email struggles to carry. The effort and thought that go into writing one tell the recipient far more than the words alone.

## When Form Matters: From Salutation to Sign-Off

A good letter also lives by its form. In a formal letter the opening sets the tone: "Dear Mr./Ms. …" when you address someone with respect, followed by the body and a considered close. In the US, "Sincerely" or "Best regards" are the standard sign-offs; in the UK, "Yours sincerely" (when you know the name) and "Yours faithfully" (when you don't) still hold. Don't forget a clean sender and recipient layout on the envelope. If you want to be sure of the details, our guide to [letter writing etiquette](/en/blog/letter-writing-etiquette) rounds up the key dos and don'ts.

## Connecting Across Distances

Letters have the power to bridge distances and strengthen relationships. Whether it's a heartfelt note to a loved one living far away or a thoughtful message to a friend across the globe, a letter transcends borders and creates a closeness that virtual communication often struggles to reach.

## When It Needs to Count: Certified and Recorded Mail

Some occasions call for more than a quick message. A cancellation, a formal complaint, or an important notice carries more weight as a letter. For correspondence with legal significance, many people deliberately choose a tracked option — **Certified Mail** with USPS in the US, or **Signed For / Recorded delivery** with Royal Mail in the UK — to document that it was sent and received. Whether a particular letter is required, and in what form, depends on the situation; when in doubt, re-read the relevant contract or check first. What's clear is that, on paper, an official matter reads as more serious and stays on the record.

## Timeless Expressions of Emotion

From love letters to notes of gratitude, the letter remains a timeless stage for expressing feelings in their purest form. Putting pen to paper — or choosing your words calmly on screen — lets you be authentic, away from the distractions of digital life. Just how much a written note can move someone comes through in our piece on the [art of love letters](/en/blog/art-love-letters).

## Slowing Down: Writing as a Deliberate Gesture

In a world flooded with notifications and fleeting messages, taking the time to write a letter is an act of mindfulness. It's an invitation to pause, reflect, and give someone your attention. That very slowness is what makes the letter valuable: not just another medium, but a visible sign of regard.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Letter Writing

### Why write letters at all when email exists?

Because a letter stands out. It gets opened, read, and often kept, while digital messages disappear into a stream of emails, chats, and notifications. For personal, emotional, or especially important occasions, the letter remains the stronger choice.

### Does a letter have to be handwritten?

No. Handwriting feels more intimate, but a clean, well-formatted printed letter is just as valuable — especially for formal or professional occasions, where legibility and a clear structure matter.

### How do I start a letter properly?

With a fitting salutation: "Dear [Name]" for a personal letter, or "Dear Mr./Ms. …" in a formal one. The reason for writing follows, and you close with a sign-off such as "Sincerely" or "Best regards."

### How much does it cost to send a letter?

Postage depends on the size and weight of your mail. Current prices — including options like Certified Mail or Signed For delivery — are published by your postal service (USPS, Royal Mail, and others); a quick check before you send always helps.

## Start Writing Letters Again

In a world awash with notifications and fleeting messages, taking the time for a letter is a deliberate choice. So why not dust off your stationery, pick up a pen — or set your words down calmly on screen — and let your thoughts flow? You might be surprised by how much a simple letter can still say, even in the digital age.
