# Letter — User Manual

**Letter** is an app for writing, formatting, and printing real letters. You compose your text, choose how it should look on the page, and the app produces a print-ready PDF you can share or send to a printer. This manual walks through everything the app can do.

> **Letter PRO:** Some features are part of the **Letter PRO** subscription (marked **PRO** below). The first time you tap a PRO feature, the app shows the upgrade screen with a free trial. Everything else — writing a letter, previewing it, sharing and printing it — is free.

## 1. Getting around

When you open the app you land on your **list of letters**.

- **Search** — Use the search field at the top to filter letters by subject or content.
- **New letter** — Tap the **+** button (bottom-right) to start a new letter.
- **Open a letter** — Tap any letter in the list to preview it.
- **Settings** — Tap the settings (gear) icon in the search bar to open app settings.

> **On iPhone/iPad:** the controls sit in the navigation bar instead — the **+** button is in the **top-right**, and the **settings** (gear) button is in the **top-left**.

On a tablet, the list stays on the left and the selected letter's preview shows on the right (see [Phones vs. tablets](#14-phones-vs-tablets)).

## 2. Writing a letter

Tap **+** to create a letter, or open an existing one and tap **Edit** (pencil icon).

The editor has three header fields and a body:

- **Sender** — your own address (the "From"). You can type it, pick a saved [sender profile](#8-senders--identities-pro), or [import a contact](#10-importing-addresses-from-contacts-pro).
- **Recipient** — who the letter is going to (the "To"). Type it or import a contact.
- **Subject** — the subject line. You can also apply a saved [template](#9-templates-pro) here.
- **Body** — the letter text itself.

### Saving

Tap the **✓ (Save)** button in the top-right to save. New letters are added to your list; edits update the existing letter and return you to the preview.

### Discarding changes

If you try to leave the editor with unsaved changes, the app asks **"Discard Changes?"** — choose **Discard** to throw them away or **Keep Editing** to go back.

### Deleting a letter

Open the letter and tap the **Delete** (trash) icon in the preview, then confirm.

## 3. Formatting text

**(PRO)** In the editor, a formatting toolbar lets you style selected text:

| Button | Shortcut | Effect |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Bold** | Ctrl+B | Bold text |
| *Italic* | Ctrl+I | Italic text |
| <u>Underline</u> | Ctrl+U | Underlined text |

Select the text you want to change, then tap the button (or use the keyboard shortcut if you have a hardware keyboard).

> If you prefer a plain letter with no styling, turn on **Text Only** in [letter settings](#6-letter-layout--settings-pro).

## 4. The AI writing assistant (PRO)

If enabled, a **sparkle (✨)** button appears in the editor. It drafts a subject and letter body for you.

**How to use it:**

1. Type at least a short note about what you want — a subject and/or a few instructions in the body (a minimum amount of text is required).
2. Tap the **✨** button.
3. **First time only:** you'll be asked to agree to *Data Processing by OpenAI* — your subject and message are sent to OpenAI to generate the draft. Choose **Accept**, **Privacy** (to read the policy), or **Decline**.
4. The assistant shows a *"Writing your letter…"* animation, then presents a suggested **subject** and **body**.
5. Tap **Use** to drop the suggestion into your editor, or **Cancel** to keep what you had.

**Good to know:**

- There's a **daily quota**. If you hit it you'll see *"AI Exhausted! 😵‍💫"* — try again the next day.
- Very long letters can't be processed (*"Letter too long"*). Shorten the text and retry.
- If the service is temporarily down you'll see *"Service on a Coffee Break! ☕"* — try again later.

## 5. Previewing, sharing & printing

Opening a letter shows a **live PDF preview** of exactly how it will print. The preview updates whenever you edit the letter.

From the preview's top bar you can:

- **Share** — send the PDF through any app (email, Drive, messaging, etc.).
- **Print** — print through Android's standard print dialog (choose a printer, paper size, copies).
- **Settings** — open this letter's [layout settings](#6-letter-layout--settings-pro).
- **Edit** — return to the editor.
- **Delete** — remove the letter.

## 6. Letter layout & settings (PRO)

Open a letter and tap the **Settings** (gear) icon to control exactly how it appears on paper. Tap **✓ (Save)** when done.

**General**
- **Show sender** — include or hide your "From" address on the page.

**Stationary**
- Upload a **PDF** to use as letterhead / background behind your text. The app shows a thumbnail preview of the first pages; tap the **✕** to remove it.

**Format**
- **Paper size** — **A4** (210 × 297 mm) or **Letter** (8.5 × 11 in).
- **Language** — the language used for the date and locale-specific formatting.

**Date**
- **Date** — pick the date shown on the letter (defaults to today).
- **Prefix** — optional text before the date (e.g. a place name).
- **Date format** — Short, Medium, Long, or Full (e.g. *1/15/25* → *Monday, January 15, 2025*).

**Alignment**
- **Sender** — Left / Center / Right.
- **Date** — Left, Right, before the recipient block, or hidden.
- **Recipient** — Left / Right.
- **Signature** — Left / Right.

**Options**
- **Fold marks** — print faint lines showing where to fold for a standard envelope.
- **Page numbers** — add page numbers in the footer.
- **Sender in window** — position the sender address to show through a window envelope.
- **Text only** — strip all formatting and layout extras for a plain letter.

**Font**
- **Font type** — Sans Serif, Serif, Monospace, or Cursive.
- **Font size** — set the point size.

**Margins**
- Set the **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom**, and **Left** margins.

> Tip: Save these preferences once in a [sender profile](#8-senders--identities-pro) so every new letter starts with your preferred layout.

## 7. Signatures

You can add a signature two ways, and they can be combined:

- **Drawn signature (PRO)** — tap the signature button to open a drawing canvas. Sign with your finger or stylus, use **Clear** to start over, then confirm. Your signature is embedded as an image.
- **Text signature** — type a closing/signature line (e.g. your name).

Use **Signature alignment** in [letter settings](#6-letter-layout--settings-pro) to place it left or right.

## 8. Senders / identities (PRO)

A **Sender** (identity) is a saved "From" address bundled with your preferred layout — so you don't re-enter everything each time.

**Manage senders:** open **Settings → Senders**.

- **Add** — tap **+** to create a new sender.
- **Search** — filter your saved senders.
- **Edit** — tap a sender to change it.

**A sender stores:**
- The **From** address (multi-line).
- **Show sender** on/off.
- A **signature** (drawn image and/or text).
- All the layout options from [letter settings](#6-letter-layout--settings-pro) (paper size, date, alignment, fonts, margins, stationary…).
- A **Default sender** flag — the default is applied automatically to new letters.

**Use a sender while writing:** in the editor, use the **select sender** action next to the *Sender* field to drop a saved profile into your letter.

---

## 9. Templates (PRO)

A **Template** is a reusable subject + body you can apply to any letter — handy for recurring letters.

**Manage templates:** open **Settings → Templates**.

- **Add** — tap **+** to create a template (PRO).
- **Search** — filter templates by subject or content.
- **Edit** — tap a template to change it. Templates use the same rich-text editor (bold/italic/underline).

**Use a template while writing:** in the editor, use the **select template** action next to the *Subject* field to fill in the subject and body from a saved template.

The app also includes some **built-in templates** to get you started.

---

## 10. Importing addresses from contacts (PRO)

Next to the **Sender** and **Recipient** fields you can pull an address straight from your phone's address book:

1. Tap the **contact** action next to the field.
2. The first time, grant **Contacts** permission when prompted. (If you declined earlier, enable it in the app's system settings — the app will remind you.)
3. Pick a contact; their address fills the field.

## 12. Letter PRO subscription

**Letter PRO** unlocks the advanced features. When you tap a PRO feature without a subscription, the upgrade screen appears.

**What PRO unlocks:**
- **Sender** profiles — enter your sender info once and reuse it.
- **Contacts** — import addresses from your address book.
- **Templates** — reusable letters for any occasion.
- **Signatures** — add handwritten/personal signatures in the app.
- **Layout** — full control over how letters look.
- Plus rich-text formatting, the AI assistant, and stationary.

**Plans & trial:** the upgrade screen offers plans (e.g. weekly, monthly, yearly, or a one-time purchase) and typically a **free trial** (e.g. "Free for 1 week, then …/year"). Prices and options are shown in your local currency.

**Restore purchases:** already subscribed on another device or after reinstalling? Tap **Restore purchases** on the upgrade screen.

> If you see *"Billing is currently not available"*, check your internet connection and make sure you're signed in to the Google Play Store.

## 13. Languages

The app is available in **7 languages**: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch. It follows your device's system language automatically, including locale-aware date formatting.

## 14. Phones vs. tablets

The layout adapts to your screen:

- **Phone** — one screen at a time. Tapping a letter opens its preview full-screen; editing and settings open as their own screens.
- **Tablet (wide screens)** — a two-pane view: your **list of letters** on the left and the **preview** on the right. Editing, settings, senders, templates, and the upgrade screen open as pop-up dialogs over the preview, so you never lose your place in the list.

---

## 15. Syncing across devices (iPhone & iPad)

> This section applies to the **iPhone/iPad** version only.

On iPhone and iPad, your letters, senders, and templates **sync automatically over iCloud**. Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device and your content stays up to date everywhere — no manual export or backup needed.

- Sync happens in the background; there's no button to press.
- Changes made on one device appear on your other devices a short while later.
- iCloud must be available and enabled on the device for syncing to work.
